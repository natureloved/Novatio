## 1. Brand Concept & Legal Origin

Novatio is derived from the Roman and modern commercial contract doctrine of **novation** (*Latin: novatio*): the formal extinguishment of an existing contractual obligation and the creation of a new, legally enforceable obligation with a new creditor upon the debtor's explicit assent.

The brand mark visually captures this transformation:
1. **Dual Contractual Endpoints:** Two solid geometric monoliths represent the original parties (the enterprise debtor and the commercial supplier).
2. **The Novation Channel:** A precision diagonal axis separates and intersects the two forms, representing the atomic transfer of the right to be paid from the supplier to the factorer.
3. **Negative-Space Monogram "N":** The interaction of the two forms and the transfer axis carves out an architectural, uppercase letter **"N"** through negative space.
4. **Architectural Stability:** Grounded, vertical geometry communicating the institutional trust of a Tier-1 clearing house, sovereign debt registry, or global market infrastructure provider (DTCC, Swift, Euroclear).

## 4. Typography

### Primary Wordmark: Neo-Grotesk
- **Typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter / Helvetica Neue (Bold 800 / Black 900)
- **Casing:** All uppercase (`NOVATIO`)
- **Tracking / Letter-Spacing:** `+0.08em` to `+0.12em` (expanded, authoritative institutional spacing)
- **Proportions:** The cap-height of the wordmark is optically aligned to 55% of the total height of the icon mark in horizontal lockups.

### Monospace Code & Financial Tickers
- **Typeface:** JetBrains Mono / SF Mono / Fira Code
- **Usage:** Contract IDs, transaction hashes, ISO 20022 identifiers, face value metrics ($100,000.00).

## 5. Composition & Lockup Standards

### 1. Primary Horizontal Lockup
`[ICON MARK]  NOVATIO`
- Minimum Clear Space: Equal to 50% of the icon width on all four sides.
- Minimum Width: 120px for digital screens, 28mm for print.

### 2. Standalone Icon Mark
`[ICON MARK]`
- Sizing Range: From 16x16px (favicon) to 1024x1024px (app store / protocol badge).
- Geometry maintains optical clarity even at 16px due to high-contrast negative space.

### 3. Monochrome Versatility
- Functions with 100% fidelity in solid black (`#000000`) on white bond paper and solid white (`#FFFFFF`) on dark surfaces.

## 6. Brand Restrictions (What to Avoid)

- **NO Crypto Clichés:** Never incorporate floating cubes, coins, blockchain nodes, rocket ships, or neon glow effects.
- **NO Generic Legal Icons:** Never use scales of justice, gavels, or handshake icons.
- **NO Skewing or Distortion:** Never stretch the icon non-proportionally.
- **NO Unapproved Colors:** Do not use purple, neon green, or warm orange as primary branding colors.
